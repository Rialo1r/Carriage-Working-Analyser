# Scope and acceptance plan

The updated build implements phases in sequence: roster management first, continuity checking second, then proposed rotations and research approval. Phase 4 file exports remain deferred.

## Roster foundation

Manual catalogues, editing and safe deletion/archive/replacement, distinct published and physical identities, daily variants, formations, weekday/date and recurring-week calendars, exact continuation selection, stabling instructions, distinct dated occurrences, audit history and weekly boards.

## Continuity checking

Location/calendar/date/time/formation checks, missing instructions, candidates, duplicate/contradictory records, allocation overlap checks and persistent issue reviews. Unknowns remain unverified. Source citations can explicitly support a continuation; station matches alone do not establish identity.

## Rotation research

Generation follows exact source instructions and manually approved links over 2–26 weeks. Candidates are not chosen arbitrarily. Explicit approval creates/associates a permanent physical set. Chains can remain open or show supported recurrence. Saved snapshots retain research, locks protect approved links/allocations, and recalculation produces separate proposals. Stabling between dated uses is visible. Multi-week boards and clickable chains derive from these records.

## Deferred reporting

Dedicated printable reports, PDF, Excel, CSV and research-summary downloads. Browser printing is available through the browser but is not a dedicated export pipeline.
